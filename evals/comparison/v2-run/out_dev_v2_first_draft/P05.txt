After 9 years in banking, I left in March to open a bakery in Leeds. It's called Crumb & Co.

The first three months were rough. We lost money every single week, and more than once I wondered if I'd made a mistake.

Then I started a Saturday sourdough subscription. Six weeks later we had 85 subscribers and revenue finally settled down.

What I took from it: keep going, change the plan when it isn't working, and pay attention to what your customers want. And if you're thinking about making a leap yourself, I'd start now.
