After 9 years in banking, I left in March and opened a bakery in Leeds. It's called Crumb & Co.

The first three months were rough. We lost money every single week, and more than once I wondered if I'd made the wrong call.

Then I started a Saturday sourdough subscription. Six weeks later we had 85 subscribers and the revenue had finally settled down. What I took from it is pretty simple: keep going, be ready to change what you're doing, and pay attention to what your customers want.
