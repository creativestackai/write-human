Remote work gives people more flexibility, a better balance between work and home, and the chance to hire from anywhere in the world. The costs are real too. Communication gets harder, some people end up feeling isolated, and company culture is difficult to hold together when nobody shares a room.

Whether a team is remote or in the office matters less than what holds it together, which is trust, openness about what everyone is doing, and people owning what they said they'd do. Give a distributed team decent tools and clear habits for talking to each other, and it can do good work.
