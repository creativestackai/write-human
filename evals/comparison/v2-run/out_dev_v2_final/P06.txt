I'd keep the fallback in this PR and open a separate ticket for the real fix. The backfill is out of scope here because it touches the customers table, and that's shared across several services.

The proper fix is a migration that backfills `currency` from each customer's billing country, then adds a NOT NULL constraint. For scale: `currency` can be null on accounts created before 2021, the export silently defaults those to USD, and that gives wrong totals for roughly 4% of European accounts. I ran a query against staging and 212 accounts are affected.
