I ran my first marathon in Pune recently and finished in 4 hours 38 minutes. It wasn't a smooth build-up. In week 10 of a 16-week plan my knee gave out, and I had to stop running for eight days. For a while I honestly didn't think I'd make it to the start line.

My running group and a physiotherapist got me back out there, and I ended up in better shape than before the injury. What I took from it is pretty plain: keep showing up, be patient with the slow stretches, and let other people help. Nothing I did that month was clever.

If you've been putting off something big, start this week, even if it's small.
