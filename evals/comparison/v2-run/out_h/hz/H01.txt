AI is changing how schools teach. Tools can now build personalized learning paths and grade work automatically, which gives teachers and students more room to do what they're good at. The problems are real too: privacy, equity, and the risk that people lean on the technology too much.

The tools work best when they extend what teachers can do, with teachers staying at the center. Schools that adopt them while keeping their limits in view can make learning more engaging, more inclusive, and more effective.
