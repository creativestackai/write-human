Northwind Dental Group wanted fewer patient no-shows across its four clinics, so it started using Remindly, a messaging platform that works with existing scheduling systems. In 90 days the no-show rate went from 18% to 11%, an estimated 340 more appointments kept.

"Remindly has been a game-changer for our front-desk team," said practice manager Elena Torres. The automated SMS reminders got more patients in the chair and freed up staff time. Northwind still uses Remindly today as a core part of how it keeps in touch with patients.
