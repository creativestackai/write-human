I recently finished my first marathon, in Pune, in 4 hours and 38 minutes. It wasn't a smooth run up to it. In week 10 of my 16-week plan I hurt my knee and had to take eight days off, and I seriously wondered whether I'd even make the starting line.

My running group and a physiotherapist are the reason I did. I came back stronger than before. What I took from it is that consistency, patience and the people around you do most of the work on any big goal.

If you've got a challenge of your own in mind, start today. What goal are you chasing?
