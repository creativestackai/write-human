AI is changing how schools approach education. Personalized learning pathways and automated grading are two examples, and both are meant to give teachers and students more room to do what they're there to do. But the tools bring problems of their own: privacy, equity, and the risk that schools come to lean on them too much.

Teachers shouldn't be replaced. AI should extend what they can do. Schools that adopt it while keeping its limits in view stand a better chance of classrooms that are more engaging and inclusive, and more effective than before.
