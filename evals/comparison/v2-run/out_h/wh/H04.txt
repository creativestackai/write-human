I ran my first marathon in Pune. 4 hours 38 minutes. Not fast, and I'm fine with that.

Week 10 of a 16-week plan, my knee gave out and I had to take eight days off. I spent most of them googling whether people ever make the start line after something like that, which is a bad way to spend eight days. I honestly thought I was done.

What got me back was my running group, who kept me showing up even when all I could do was watch, and a physiotherapist who had me doing the same dull knee exercises until I wanted to scream. Nothing clever. I did the boring work, the knee came round, and I was on the line.

If you've got something you keep putting off, start before you feel ready. I wasn't ready. I'd just stopped waiting.
