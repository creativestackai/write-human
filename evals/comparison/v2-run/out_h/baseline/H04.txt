I finished my first marathon in Pune. 4:38. Not fast, but I'll take it.

Week 10 of a 16-week plan is when my knee gave out. Eight days off. I spent most of them googling whether I'd still make the start line, and I honestly didn't think I would.

My running group kept dragging me to the Sunday meetups even though all I could do was watch, and a physio worked out what was actually wrong. When I started running again I kept it slow and boring, and that's the only reason I got to race day.

What I took from it is that showing up most weeks beats a perfect plan. Also that I'm bad at asking for help and should fix that.
