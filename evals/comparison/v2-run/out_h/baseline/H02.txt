Northwind Dental Group has four clinics and a no-show problem. They started using Remindly, a text-message reminder tool that plugs into the scheduling software they already had. After 90 days, the no-show rate had gone from 18% to 11%. Northwind estimates that's about 340 more appointments kept.

"Remindly has been a game-changer for our front-desk team," said practice manager Elena Torres. The reminders go out as automated texts, so patients show up more often and the front desk has less to do by hand. Northwind still uses it today.
