I taught for 11 years, and now I'm a UX designer.

Getting here meant an 8-month part-time bootcamp on top of a full-time teaching job, so a lot of late nights and early mornings. In January I accepted my first design role, at a fintech startup in Bengaluru. It came with a 40% pay cut.

I haven't looked back. What I learned is that it isn't too late to start over, and that the skills you already have carry across further than you'd expect.
