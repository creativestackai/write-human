Motivation comes and goes, so it makes a poor thing to build on. Consistency is what lasts. Do something small every day for weeks, then months, and it compounds into results much bigger than any single day would suggest.

That's also why willpower is the wrong tool. You'll run out of it. A better bet is a system that makes the good behavior automatic, so you aren't deciding each time. Start small and keep track of what you actually do. Be patient with yourself while it adds up.
