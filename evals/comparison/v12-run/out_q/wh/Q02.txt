Motivation is unreliable. You have plenty of it on Monday and none by Thursday, so any plan that depends on it was going to fail anyway.

What works is boring. Do something small every day, and keep doing it after it stops feeling interesting. Ten pushups, or one page of the book you keep meaning to finish. A few months of that adds up to more than any single big push, mostly because you're still showing up.

Don't count on willpower to get you there. Set things up so the right choice is the easy one: running shoes by the door, phone charging in another room. Start smaller than feels worthwhile, write down each day you did it, and when you miss one, do the next day. Missing a day barely matters. Quitting does.

That's about all there is to it. Pick one thing and do it tomorrow.
