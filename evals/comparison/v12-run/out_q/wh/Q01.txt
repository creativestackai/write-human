After eleven years in the classroom, I'm a UX designer now. Still weird to type.

The bootcamp ran eight months, part-time, and I did it while teaching full time. So: late nights, early mornings, and a lot of coffee I'd rather not think about. I wouldn't recommend the schedule. I'd do it again anyway.

In January I started my first design job, at a fintech startup in Bengaluru. It pays 40% less than teaching did. Yes, I did the maths. Yes, I signed.

It's been hard, and I haven't once wanted to go back. The bit nobody warned me about is how much of the job I'd already been doing for years. Working out why someone is confused, then explaining it another way, then watching their face to see if it worked. A room of Year 6s is the toughest usability test you'll ever sit through, and I ran one every day.

So if you're thinking about a move like this, work out the pay cut first and be honest about it. Then go.
