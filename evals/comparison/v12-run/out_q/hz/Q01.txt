After 11 years in the classroom, I've left teaching for UX design.

I did an 8-month part-time bootcamp while still teaching full time, which meant a lot of late nights and early mornings. It was tiring, and I won't pretend otherwise. In January I started my first design job at a fintech startup in Bengaluru, and it came with a 40% pay cut. That stung. I took it anyway.

I haven't looked back. It's not too late to start over, and the skills I built as a teacher came with me. If you're thinking about a change like this, trust yourself and make the jump. What's the thing you keep putting off?
