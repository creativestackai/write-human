After 11 years in the classroom, I've left teaching and become a UX designer.

It wasn't easy. I did an 8-month part-time bootcamp while still teaching full time, which meant late nights and early mornings. Then in January I started my first design job, at a fintech startup in Bengaluru. It came with a 40% pay cut.

I haven't looked back once.

If you ask me what I learned, it's that it isn't too late to start over, and the skills you already have come with you. So if you're thinking about changing careers, take the leap. What's holding you back?
