Motivation comes and goes, so don't build on it. Habits are what last. A small thing done every day for weeks and then months adds up to far more than any single push.

Willpower won't carry you either. What works is a system that makes the good behavior happen almost without thinking. Start small. Keep track of what you do. And be patient with yourself, because change this way is slow, and slow is fine.

Every step still counts toward the goal. So start today.
