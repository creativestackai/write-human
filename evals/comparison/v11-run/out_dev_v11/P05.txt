After 9 years in banking, I left in March and opened a bakery in Leeds. It's called Crumb & Co.

The first three months were rough. We lost money every single week, and more than once I wondered what on earth I'd done.

Then I started a Saturday sourdough subscription. Six weeks later we had 85 subscribers and the revenue finally held steady.

What I took from it: keep going, change what isn't working, and listen to your customers. If you're thinking about making a leap, the best time to start is now. What's holding you back?
