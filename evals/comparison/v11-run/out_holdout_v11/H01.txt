AI is changing how schools teach. Personalized learning paths and automated grading are the obvious examples, and both can help teachers and students get more out of their time. The costs are real, though. Privacy is a worry, equity is a worry, and so is schools leaning on the technology harder than they should.

The way through is to use AI to extend what teachers can do, not to replace them. A school that adopts it with its limits in mind can end up with classrooms that are more engaging, more inclusive and more effective.
