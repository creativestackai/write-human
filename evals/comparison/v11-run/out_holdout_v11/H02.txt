Northwind Dental Group wanted to cut patient no-shows across its four clinics, so it brought in Remindly, a messaging platform that plugs into existing scheduling systems. Over 90 days, the no-show rate fell from 18% to 11%. That works out to an estimated 340 more appointments kept.

"Remindly has been a game-changer for our front-desk team," said practice manager Elena Torres. The reminders go out by SMS, automatically. More patients showed up, and staff got time back. Northwind still uses it.
