I finished my first marathon in Pune. 4 hours 38 minutes.

It wasn't a clean build-up. In week 10 of my 16-week plan my knee gave out, I had to take eight days off, and I seriously doubted I'd make the start line at all.

My running group and a physiotherapist are why I did. I came back stronger than before.

What I took from it is pretty simple. Consistency and patience matter more than I'd have said going in, and you need people around you. Any big goal sits on those.

If you've been circling one of your own, take the first step today. What are you chasing?
