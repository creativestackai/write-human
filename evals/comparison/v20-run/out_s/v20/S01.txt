Right. I've sold Kettle & Thread, my café in Pune, after six years. It wasn't an easy call and it was a hell of a ride.

We opened in 2019 with four tables and a second-hand espresso machine. At our peak we were serving 300 customers a day. Then the rents kept climbing until the business couldn't carry them, and in August the lease went to a new owner.

What I took from it: passion, perseverance and community are what matter. If you're thinking of starting something of your own, that's the part I'd hold onto.
