Phones should be put away during lessons by default, with teachers free to allow them for specific tasks. A total ban is the wrong call, and so is total freedom. Phones are a constant source of distraction, and taking them away removes a temptation few teenagers can resist on their own, so a ban does help concentration. But phones are also a tool for looking things up, and a blanket ban treats every student as though they can't be trusted with one.
