Focus has become a rare skill, and a valuable one, now that notifications never stop and the scrolling never ends. Attention isn't just a resource. It's what meaningful work is built on.

Get pulled in enough directions and you trade depth for breadth, and your best ideas never get the time to develop. Willpower alone won't fix that. Focus comes from designing an environment that supports sustained concentration: set boundaries, cut the distractions, put what matters first. Anyone can win their attention back that way, and it starts with one deliberate choice.
