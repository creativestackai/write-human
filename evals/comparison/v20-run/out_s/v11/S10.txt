Banning phones in classrooms looks like an easy way to get students concentrating. It's less simple than that. Phones are a constant distraction, and taking them away removes a temptation few teenagers can resist on their own. But a phone is also a way to look things up, and a blanket ban treats every student as someone who can't be trusted with one. So the best policy is neither a total ban nor total freedom. Phones should be put away during lessons by default, and teachers should be free to allow them for specific tasks.
