Moving the warehouse from two 10-hour shifts to three 8-hour shifts means more handovers each day, which is a fair concern. The September trial at Nashik complicates it. Over four weeks, picking errors fell from 2.4% to 1.7%, which suggests shorter shifts cut fatigue-related mistakes. Overtime costs rose 11% over the same period, though, and that points to a third shift that was not fully staffed.

Taken together, the results support adopting the three-shift pattern at Nashik, provided six additional pickers are hired before the December peak.
