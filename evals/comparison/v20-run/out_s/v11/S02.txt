Between the notifications and the endless scrolling, being able to focus has turned into a rare skill, and a valuable one. Attention isn't just a resource you spend. Meaningful work rests on it. Let yourself get pulled in every direction and you trade depth for breadth, and your best ideas never get the time to grow.

Willpower alone won't fix that. What helps is building an environment that makes sustained concentration easier: set boundaries, cut the distractions, and decide what actually matters. Anyone can take their attention back this way.

It starts with one deliberate choice.
