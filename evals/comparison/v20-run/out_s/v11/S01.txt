After six years, I've sold Kettle & Thread, my café in Pune. It was not an easy call.

We opened in 2019 with 4 tables and a second-hand espresso machine. At our peak we were serving 300 customers a day. Then the rent kept climbing until the numbers stopped working, and in August the lease went to a new owner.

I learned a lot, and some of it I'm still sorting out. The short version is that passion, perseverance and the people around you matter more than anything else I can name.

If you're thinking about starting something of your own, do it. What are you putting off?
