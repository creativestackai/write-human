Right. News. After six years, I've sold Kettle & Thread, my café in Pune. In August the lease went to a new owner.

We opened in 2019 with four tables and a second-hand espresso machine. At the peak we were serving 300 people a day, which still sounds made up when I say it. Then the rent kept climbing, and at some point the numbers just stopped working. I'd love to tell you there was a clever way out. There wasn't.

I'm not going to wrap this up with a lesson about passion and community. I'm still sad, and I'm also oddly relieved, and I haven't worked out how those two sit together. If you've got something you keep putting off, though, I'd say go and start it. Just read the lease first.
