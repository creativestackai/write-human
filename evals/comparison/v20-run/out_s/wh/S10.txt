Banning phones in classrooms sounds like an easy way to help students concentrate. It's less simple than that. Phones are a constant distraction, and a ban removes a temptation most teenagers can't resist alone. But they're also how a student looks up a word or checks a date, and a blanket ban treats everyone as if they can't be trusted with one. Neither a total ban nor total freedom seems right to me. I'd have phones put away during lessons by default and let teachers allow them for specific tasks.
