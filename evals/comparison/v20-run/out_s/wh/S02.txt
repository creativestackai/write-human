My phone buzzes about once a minute, and I've noticed I can no longer read three pages of anything without reaching for it. Focus used to be ordinary. Now it's rare, and I think that makes it worth a lot.

Attention is what the work is made of. If you get pulled in ten directions, you end up with ten shallow starts and no finished idea, because the good ideas tend to show up late, after you've stayed with a problem longer than is comfortable.

Willpower won't fix that. I've tried, and it lasts about an hour. What works better is changing the room: phone in another room, notifications off, one task on the screen. It's dull advice. It also works.
