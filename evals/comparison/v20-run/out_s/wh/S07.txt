We surveyed 1,840 undergraduates at three universities to see how sleep relates to grades. Students who slept fewer than six hours a night had a mean GPA 0.31 points lower than those who slept seven to eight. The gap held after we controlled for part-time work and course load. It was larger for first-years. We think this is a case for universities to act on sleep directly, for example by targeting support at first-year students, though a survey like this cannot show that short sleep causes the lower grades.
