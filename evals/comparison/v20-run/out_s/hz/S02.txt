Between the notifications and the endless scrolling, being able to focus is getting rare. And I'd say attention is what meaningful work is made of, not just something you spend. If you let yourself get pulled in ten directions, you trade depth for breadth, and your best ideas never get the time they need to grow up.

Willpower won't fix that on its own. What helps is arranging your surroundings so concentrating is the easy thing to do: put your phone in another room, close the tabs, decide in advance what matters today and ignore the rest. Anyone can get their attention back this way. It starts when you pick one thing and stay with it.
