Banning phones in classrooms sounds like an easy way to help students concentrate, but I don't think it's that simple. Phones are a constant distraction, and few teenagers can resist one sitting in their pocket. They're also useful for looking things up, and a blanket ban assumes no student can be trusted with one. So I'd go with neither extreme. Phones stay put away during lessons by default, and teachers can allow them when a task needs one.
