Webhook Retries

If your endpoint doesn't return a `2xx` status code within 10 seconds, we treat the delivery as failed and retry it, up to 5 times over 24 hours.

Every request carries an `X-Signature` header. Use it to check that the payload came from us.

Make your handler idempotent, because the same event can arrive more than once. Compare the `event_id` field against events you've already processed to catch duplicates.
