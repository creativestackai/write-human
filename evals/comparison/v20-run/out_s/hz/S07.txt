We looked at how sleep relates to grades among undergraduates, using survey data from 1,840 students at three universities. Students who slept fewer than six hours a night had a mean GPA 0.31 points lower than those who slept seven to eight hours. The gap held up after we controlled for part-time work and course load. It was larger for first-year students. Sleep seems to matter for academic success, and universities may want to target support at students who are short on it.
