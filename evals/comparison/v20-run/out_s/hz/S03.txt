Moving the warehouse from two 10-hour shifts to three 8-hour shifts means more handovers each day, and that was my first worry. The September trial at Nashik cuts against it, though. Over four weeks, picking errors fell from 2.4% to 1.7%, which suggests shorter shifts reduce fatigue-related mistakes. Overtime costs rose 11% over the same period, which suggests the third shift wasn't fully staffed. So I'd adopt the three-shift pattern at Nashik, as long as we hire six more pickers before the December peak.
