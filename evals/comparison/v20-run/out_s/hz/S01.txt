I sold Kettle & Thread. Six years running my café in Pune, and in August the lease went to a new owner.

We opened in 2019 with four tables and a second-hand espresso machine that groaned every time you asked it for anything. At our peak we were doing 300 customers a day, which still feels like a number I made up. Then the rent kept climbing until the maths stopped working. I tried. It didn't.

I'm not going to pretend I've drawn some neat lesson from it. Mostly I'm tired, and a bit sad, and weirdly fine at the same time. If you're sitting on a thing you've always wanted to start, start it. Just read the lease properly first. What's yours?
