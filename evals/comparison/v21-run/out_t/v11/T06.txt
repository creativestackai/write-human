Requiring two reviewers on every pull request looks like an easy way to catch more bugs. It's less clear than it seems. A second reviewer may notice what the first missed, especially in parts of the codebase they don't know. But when two people are responsible, each can assume the other looked carefully, and reviews get slower without getting better. So the rule probably helps most where changes are risky and least where they're routine. Require two reviewers only for payment and authentication code. One is enough everywhere else.
