Moving the weekly farmers' market from Saturday to Sunday would avoid the clash with the football fixtures that close Bridge Street. The stallholder survey complicates that. Of the 23 stallholders, 14 said they could trade on Sundays, 6 said they could not, and 3 did not reply. That looks viable. But the six who can't include both bakers, and it's unclear whether replacements could be found. The evidence doesn't yet support a decision either way. The sensible move is to ask the council to defer the vote until the bakers' position is known.
