Raising the Pro plan from $29 to $35 a month should bring in more revenue per customer. The March test complicates that. Among the 2,000 trial users shown the higher price, conversion fell from 6.1% to 5.4%. That drop is smaller than the price rise, so revenue would still go up overall. But the test ran for only three weeks, and it may miss customers who cancel later. So the higher price is probably worth adopting for new customers, provided churn is monitored for at least two quarters before it is extended to existing ones.
