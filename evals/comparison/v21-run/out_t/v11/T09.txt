Learning a language as an adult is hard, though rewarding, and it doesn't happen overnight. Fluency comes from practicing a little every day. Many learners focus only on grammar and neglect speaking and listening, which is what real conversation runs on. The fear of making mistakes also keeps people from practicing with native speakers. Treat errors as part of the process, set goals you can actually reach, and get into the language whenever you can. Talent matters less than persistence.
