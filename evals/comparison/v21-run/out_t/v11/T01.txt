Pulling off the old tiles showed what was behind them: the wall at the back of the shower was in a bad way. The plasterboard had gone over about two square metres, so all of it had to come out and be replaced with moisture-resistant board. Nobody had planned for that. It added £340 to the budget and two weekends to the schedule. And then the waterproofing membrane on the new board needed 48 hours to cure before any tiling could start.
