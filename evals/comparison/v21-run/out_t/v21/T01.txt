Once the old tiles were off, the wall behind them turned out to be wrecked by water. The plasterboard behind the shower had rotted across about two square metres, so all of it had to come out and be replaced with moisture-resistant board. None of that was in the plan. It added £340 and two weekends. Proper preparation, it turns out, is what any renovation hangs on. And then the waterproofing membrane over the new board needed 48 hours to cure before tiling could start.
