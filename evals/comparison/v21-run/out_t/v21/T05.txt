Raising the Pro plan from $29 to $35 a month increases revenue per customer. The March test complicates that: among the 2,000 trial users shown the higher price, conversion fell from 6.1% to 5.4%. That suggests the increase would still raise revenue overall, since the price rises by more than conversion falls. But the test ran for only three weeks, and it may not capture customers who cancel later. So the higher price is probably worth adopting for new customers, provided churn is monitored for at least two quarters before it is extended to existing ones.
