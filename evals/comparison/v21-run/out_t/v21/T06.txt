Requiring two reviewers on every pull request looks like an easy way to catch more bugs, but it's less clear than that. A second reviewer may notice what the first missed, particularly in unfamiliar parts of the codebase. When two people are responsible, though, each can assume the other looked carefully, and reviews may get slower without getting better. So the rule is likely to help most on risky changes and least on routine ones. That suggests requiring two reviewers probably only for changes to payment and authentication code, with one reviewer enough everywhere else.
