Learning a language as an adult is rewarding and hard, and it takes dedication and patience. Nobody gets fluent overnight. It comes from practicing a little every day.

A common trap is spending all your time on grammar and neglecting speaking and listening, which is what real conversation runs on. The fear of making mistakes also holds a lot of people back from practicing with native speakers. Treat errors as a normal part of the process, set goals you can actually reach, and get into the language whenever you can.

What gets you there is persistence, not talent.
