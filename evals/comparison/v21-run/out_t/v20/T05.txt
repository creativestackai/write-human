Raise the Pro plan from $29 to $35 a month, but watch churn before rolling it out widely. The higher price brings in more per customer, and the March test suggests it brings in more overall: among the 2,000 trial users shown $35, conversion fell from 6.1% to 5.4%, which is a smaller drop than the price rise. The caveat is that the test ran only three weeks, so it may miss customers who cancel later. Adopt the new price for new customers, and monitor churn for at least two quarters before extending it to existing ones.
