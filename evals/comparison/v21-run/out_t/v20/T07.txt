The council should be asked to defer the vote until the bakers' position is known. Moving the weekly farmers' market from Saturday to Sunday would avoid the clash with the football fixtures that close Bridge Street, and the stallholder survey partly backs it: 14 of the 23 stallholders said they could trade on Sundays. But 6 said they could not, and 3 did not reply. Those six include both bakers, and it is unclear whether replacements could be found. The evidence doesn't yet support a decision either way.
