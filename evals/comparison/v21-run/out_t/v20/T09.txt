Fluency doesn't come overnight. It comes from practicing a little every day, and what decides the outcome is persistence, not talent.

Learning a language as an adult is rewarding and hard, and it takes dedication, patience, and the right mindset. Many learners pour their time into grammar and neglect speaking and listening, which is what real communication runs on. Plenty also avoid practicing with native speakers because they're afraid of making mistakes. Treat the mistakes as part of the process. Set goals you can actually reach, and get into the language whenever you can.
