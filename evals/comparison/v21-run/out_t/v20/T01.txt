Once the old tiles were off, the wall behind them turned out to be badly water damaged. The plasterboard behind the shower had rotted across about two square metres, so all of it had to come out and be replaced with moisture-resistant board. That was an extra £340 and two more weekends, and a good reminder that proper preparation matters. Then the waterproofing membrane on the new board needed 48 hours to cure before any tiling could start.
