From a foundational standpoint, raising the Pro plan from $29 to $35 a month increases revenue per customer. Upon closer examination, however, the March test complicates this expectation: among the 2,000 trial users shown the higher price, conversion fell from 6.1% to 5.4%. On the one hand, this suggests that the increase would still raise revenue overall, since the price rises by more than conversion falls. On the other hand, the test ran for only three weeks, and it may not capture customers who cancel later. Taken together, these results indicate that the higher price is probably worth adopting for new customers, provided that churn is monitored for at least two quarters before it is extended to existing ones.
