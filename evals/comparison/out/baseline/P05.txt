I left banking in March after nine years and opened a bakery in Leeds. It's called Crumb & Co.

The first three months were bad. We lost money every single week, and more than once I sat in the shop after closing wondering if I'd made a huge mistake.

What turned it around was a Saturday sourdough subscription. I almost didn't bother with it. But six weeks in we had 85 subscribers, and for the first time the numbers stopped bleeding. People wanted the same loaf every week without having to think about it, and I'd been too busy trying to sell them everything else to notice.

If you're sitting on a leap of your own, I can't tell you when to take it. I can tell you that I'd have started earlier. What's the thing you keep putting off?
