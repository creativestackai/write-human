We got to Lisbon airport around 5:40, and the guy at the Ryanair desk, who I swear was about fifteen, tells us our bags are four kilos over. Four! My wife had weighed them on the bathroom scale that very morning. So there we were, sat on the floor by the check-in queue, cramming jumpers into our coat pockets. A woman behind us offered to carry my hiking boots in her tote, since she was flying business or something. I said no. Pride, basically. I wore them instead, through the whole 14-hour connection in Dublin, sweating like a horse. Never again. Next time I'll just pay the stupid fee.
