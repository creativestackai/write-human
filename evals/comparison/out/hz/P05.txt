I left banking in March after 9 years and opened a bakery in Leeds called Crumb & Co. The first three months were rough. We lost money every single week, and more than once I wondered if I'd made a mistake.

What turned it around was a Saturday sourdough subscription. Six weeks after I started offering it we had 85 subscribers, and the revenue finally evened out. The main thing I took from it is to listen to your customers, because they were telling me what they wanted before I caught on. If you're thinking about making a leap yourself, don't wait too long.
