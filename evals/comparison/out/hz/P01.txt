Remote work gives people more flexibility and a better balance between work and life, and it lets companies hire from anywhere instead of one city. It also has real costs. Communication gets harder, people can feel isolated, and company culture is difficult to keep intact when the team is spread out.

Whether a company is remote or in-person matters less than whether people trust each other, share information openly, and own their results. With tools that fit the team and clear habits for how people talk to each other, a distributed team can work well.
