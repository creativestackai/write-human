Nine years in banking, and in March I quit to open a bakery. It's called Crumb & Co. and it's in Leeds.

The first three months were rough. We lost money every single week, and more than once I stood in the shop wondering what on earth I'd done.

Then I started a Saturday sourdough subscription. Six weeks in we had 85 subscribers and the weekly numbers stopped going the wrong way. Nothing clever about it. Turns out people like knowing their bread is sorted.

If you're sitting on a leap of your own, I can't tell you when to take it. But I can tell you I'd have slept better in those first three months if I'd tried the subscription sooner.
